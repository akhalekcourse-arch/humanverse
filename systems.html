Body Systems

The 11 major systems that maintain homeostasis and support life.

Skeletal System

Provides structure, protects organs, and enables movement through joints. Composed of 206 bones in adults.

Muscular System

Enables movement, maintains posture, and generates heat. Includes skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle.

Nervous System

Coordinates responses, processes information, and controls voluntary and involuntary actions.

Cardiovascular System

Transports blood, nutrients, gases, and wastes via the heart and blood vessels.

Respiratory System

Facilitates gas exchange — oxygen in, carbon dioxide out — through the lungs and airways.

Digestive System

Breaks down food into nutrients that can be absorbed and used by cells.

Endocrine System

Regulates body functions through hormones produced by glands.

Urinary System

Removes waste and maintains fluid and electrolyte balance via the kidneys.

Immune & Lymphatic

Defends against pathogens and returns interstitial fluid to the bloodstream.

Integumentary System

Skin, hair, and nails — protects the body and regulates temperature.

Reproductive System

Produces gametes and supports the development of offspring.
