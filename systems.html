Body Systems

The 11 major systems that maintain homeostasis and support life.

Skeletal System

Anatomy: 206 bones in adults, cartilage, ligaments.
Functions: Support, protection, movement, mineral storage, blood cell production.
Key organs: Skull, vertebrae, femur, ribs.

Muscular System

Anatomy: Skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscle tissue.
Functions: Movement, posture, heat production.
Key organs: Biceps, diaphragm, heart muscle.

Nervous System

Anatomy: Brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves.
Functions: Sensory processing, motor control, cognition.
Key organs: Cerebrum, cerebellum, spinal cord.

Cardiovascular System

Anatomy: Heart, arteries, veins, capillaries.
Functions: Transport of blood, oxygen, nutrients, waste.
Key organs: Heart (4 chambers).

Respiratory System

Anatomy: Nasal cavity, trachea, bronchi, lungs, alveoli.
Functions: Gas exchange (O₂ in, CO₂ out).
Key organs: Lungs.

Digestive System

Anatomy: Mouth to anus, liver, pancreas, gallbladder.
Functions: Ingestion, digestion, absorption, elimination.
Key organs: Stomach, small intestine, liver.

Endocrine System

Anatomy: Glands that secrete hormones into blood.
Functions: Regulation of growth, metabolism, reproduction.
Key organs: Pituitary, thyroid, adrenal, pancreas.

Urinary System

Anatomy: Kidneys, ureters, bladder, urethra.
Functions: Waste removal, fluid and electrolyte balance.
Key organs: Kidneys.

Immune & Lymphatic

Anatomy: Lymph nodes, spleen, thymus, white blood cells.
Functions: Defense against pathogens, fluid return.
Key organs: Spleen, lymph nodes.

Integumentary System

Anatomy: Skin (epidermis, dermis), hair, nails, glands.
Functions: Protection, temperature regulation, sensation.
Key organs: Skin.

Reproductive System

Anatomy: Gonads, ducts, external genitalia (male/female).
Functions: Gamete production, hormone secretion, support of offspring.
Key organs: Testes / ovaries.
