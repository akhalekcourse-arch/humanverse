About HUMANVERSE

A scientific educational digital project.

Mission

HUMANVERSE is an interactive educational platform dedicated to explaining how the human body works, how its systems function, and the chemical processes that sustain life.


Purpose

Created to demonstrate scientific research skills, biology and chemistry knowledge, digital product development, English scientific communication, and independent learning. Suitable for university portfolio presentation.


Scientific Approach

Content is based on established open educational resources (NCBI Bookshelf, OpenStax, MedlinePlus, WHO, PubMed). Facts, hypotheses, and interpretations are clearly distinguished. This material is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice or a substitute for professional healthcare.


Current Status (Phase 02)

Includes: full navigation, expanded body systems with anatomy/functions/organs, interactive anatomy explorer with search, biochemistry section, three literature-based research articles, interactive quiz with explanations, science journal, dark/light mode, SEO metadata, and responsive design.


Future Improvements

3D anatomy viewer, individual organ deep-dive pages, progress tracking, glossary, and additional original research.
