About HUMANVERSE

A scientific educational digital project.

Mission

HUMANVERSE is an interactive educational platform dedicated to explaining how the human body works, how its systems function, and the chemical processes that sustain life.


Purpose

Created to demonstrate research skills, scientific communication, technical development, and independent learning. Intended for university portfolio presentation.


Scientific Approach

Content is based on established scientific sources (NCBI Bookshelf, OpenStax, MedlinePlus, WHO, PubMed). Facts, hypotheses, and interpretations are clearly distinguished. This is educational material, not medical advice.


Development

Phase 01: Foundation website with core pages and interactive SVG anatomy explorer. Future phases will include original research articles, quizzes, progress tracking, and 3D models.
