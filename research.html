Research 01 — The Chemistry of Human Bones

Research question: What is the molecular composition of bone matrix and how does mineralization occur?

Methodology: Literature review of peer-reviewed sources (NCBI Bookshelf, OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology).

Key findings: Bone matrix consists of ~70% inorganic hydroxyapatite [Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂] and ~30% organic collagen type I. Osteoblasts secrete osteoid which then mineralizes. Calcium and phosphate homeostasis is regulated by parathyroid hormone, calcitonin, and vitamin D.

Limitations: Theoretical literature-based study; no original experimental data.

References: OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology (2022); NCBI Bookshelf — Bone Histology.

Research 02 — How Blood Transports Oxygen

Research question: How does hemoglobin structure enable efficient oxygen binding and release?

Methodology: Literature synthesis of respiratory physiology sources.

Key findings: Hemoglobin is a tetramer (α₂β₂) with four heme groups. Cooperative binding produces the sigmoid oxygen-dissociation curve. Bohr effect (lower pH, higher CO₂) facilitates oxygen unloading in tissues. ~98.5% of oxygen is transported bound to hemoglobin.

References: OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology; Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology (selected chapters).

Research 03 — The Energy of Life (ATP & Mitochondria)

Research question: How do mitochondria generate the majority of cellular ATP?

Methodology: Review of cellular respiration pathways from standard biochemistry texts.

Key findings: Complete oxidation of one glucose yields ~30–32 ATP. The electron transport chain and oxidative phosphorylation in the inner mitochondrial membrane produce the majority. Proton gradient drives ATP synthase. Oxygen is the final electron acceptor.

References: OpenStax Biology / Biochemistry; Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry (selected sections).
