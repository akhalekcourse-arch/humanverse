Knowledge Test

Test your understanding of human biology. Answers and explanations appear after submission.

1. Which organ pumps blood throughout the body?

Correct: Heart. It has four chambers and maintains systemic and pulmonary circulation.

2. What is the primary energy currency of the cell?

Correct: ATP (adenosine triphosphate). Produced mainly in mitochondria.

3. Which system is responsible for gas exchange?

Correct: Respiratory system. Alveoli in the lungs perform gas exchange.

4. Hydroxyapatite is the main inorganic component of:

Correct: Bone. Hydroxyapatite [Ca₁₀(PO₄)₆(OH)₂] provides hardness.
