Biochemistry

The chemistry of life — molecules that sustain the human body.

Proteins

Polymers of amino acids. Serve as enzymes, structural components, transporters, and signaling molecules.

Carbohydrates

Primary energy source. Include glucose, glycogen, and complex polysaccharides.

Lipids

Fats and oils for energy storage, membrane structure, and hormone synthesis.

DNA & RNA

Nucleic acids that store and transmit genetic information and direct protein synthesis.

Enzymes

Biological catalysts that accelerate chemical reactions in cells without being consumed.

Hormones

Chemical messengers produced by endocrine glands that regulate physiological processes.

ATP & Cellular Energy

Adenosine triphosphate — the universal energy currency of the cell, produced mainly in mitochondria.

Minerals & Vitamins

Essential cofactors and micronutrients required for enzyme function and overall health.
