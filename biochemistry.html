Biochemistry

The chemistry of life — molecules and reactions that sustain the human body.

Proteins

Polymers of amino acids. Functions: enzymes, structural support (collagen), transport (hemoglobin), signaling, immune defense. Folding determines function.

Carbohydrates

Primary energy source. Glucose is metabolized via glycolysis and the citric acid cycle. Glycogen is the storage form in liver and muscle.

Lipids

Fats, phospholipids, steroids. Energy storage, cell membrane structure, hormone precursors (steroid hormones), insulation.

DNA & RNA

DNA stores genetic information in the nucleus. RNA (mRNA, tRNA, rRNA) carries instructions and participates in protein synthesis (transcription and translation).

Enzymes

Biological catalysts that lower activation energy. Highly specific. Activity regulated by pH, temperature, inhibitors, and allosteric effectors.

Hormones

Chemical messengers (peptide, steroid, amine). Produced by endocrine glands. Regulate metabolism, growth, reproduction, and stress responses.

ATP & Cellular Energy

Adenosine triphosphate is the universal energy currency. Produced mainly by oxidative phosphorylation in mitochondria. Hydrolysis powers cellular work.

Vitamins & Minerals

Essential micronutrients. Vitamins act as coenzymes; minerals (Ca, Fe, Na, K, Mg) serve structural and catalytic roles.

Cellular Respiration

Process converting glucose + O₂ into CO₂, H₂O, and ATP. Stages: glycolysis, pyruvate oxidation, citric acid cycle, electron transport chain.
